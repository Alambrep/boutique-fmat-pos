# Rubric — Delivery 1 (20%)

Scale: Missing (0) · Inadequate (1) · Poor (2) · Borderline (3) · Good (4) · Excellent (5)

| # | Criterion | What is assessed | Artifacts that cover it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Definition of the project | Social relevance (arguments and evidence), innovation compared to previous developments, feasibility (strengths, weaknesses, HCI/product challenges) | `docs/01-definition/` |
| 2 | Project Process: User Research | Research (data, instruments, analysis, results); user modeling (profiles, personas, scenarios based on research; primary and secondary users) | `docs/02-research/`, `docs/03-user-modeling/` |
| 3 | Planning and repository | Schedule, task assignment, artifacts and products; organized and documented repository; essential elements of a product development process | `README.md`, `docs/00-management/` |
| 4 | Product Requirements | Functional and non-functional requirements based on usability attributes | `docs/04-requirements/` |
| 5 | Presentation | Quality of materials, formatting, timing, speech quality | `docs/05-presentation/` |
| 6 | Collaborative Work | Evidence of individual contribution (logbook, responsibilities, tasks) and a quantifiable metric | `docs/00-management/`, git history, issues |
