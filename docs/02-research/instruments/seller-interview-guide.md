# Instrument — Seller interview guide (V-02)

> Semi-structured interview, 20–30 min. Administered in Spanish; this English text is the reference version; the Spanish version used in the field is [`es/guia-entrevista-vendedores.md`](es/guia-entrevista-vendedores.md). Hypotheses: H-01, H-02, H-05, H-06, H-08, H-12, H-14.

## Consent script (interviews)
Read aloud before starting. Unlike the observation, an interview may be **audio-recorded**, only with explicit consent (author's decision, 2026-10-31: it saves time for the participant and the interviewer; it is transcribed afterwards).

"I am a Software Engineering student at FMAT working on a course project to improve the boutique's sales and inventory system. I would like to ask you some questions about your work; it takes 20–30 minutes. Your answers are used only for this course project. If you agree, I would like to record **only the audio** of the interview, so I do not have to write everything down while we talk; if you prefer that I do not record, I will only take notes. I will not record your name: in the notes and the transcript you appear only by a code. The recording is kept in my private files and deleted as soon as I have transcribed it; the notes are deleted when the course ends. Only anonymized summaries go into the project repository. You can stop, or ask me to stop the recording, at any time without giving a reason. If you have questions later, you can contact me or the course professor. Is that OK?"

## A. Role and experience (H-12)
1. How long have you been selling at the boutique? Do other people also cover this point?
2. How did you learn to do the sales and records? Did anyone train you?

## B. Technology use (H-01, H-02)
3. What do you usually use your phone for? (calls, messaging, social media, banking, other apps)
4. Have you used any app for work (sales, inventory, payments)? How was it?
5. When an app does not work as you expect, what do you do?
6. Which phone would you use to sell (yours, one from the boutique)? *(also covered in V-05)*

## C. The sales process (H-05, H-06)
7. Walk me through the last sale you made, step by step.
8. When are the busiest times? What happens when there is a queue?
9. Tell me about a recent sale that did not go as expected. What happened?

## D. Interuady (H-08, H-14)
10. How often do you receive Interuady payments? What data do you record? What happens when some of that data is missing?

## E. Closing
11. If you could change one thing about how sales are recorded, what would it be?
12. May I contact you later to try a prototype? (yes/no — no contact data stored in the repository)
