# Instrument — Staff and client interview guide (V-03, V-04, V-06)

> Semi-structured interview, 30–45 min, with CDU staff, Central Administration and the client. Administered in Spanish; this English text is the reference version; the Spanish version used in the field is [`es/guia-entrevista-personal.md`](es/guia-entrevista-personal.md).

## Consent script (interviews)
Read aloud before starting. Unlike the observation, an interview may be **audio-recorded**, only with explicit consent (author's decision, 2026-10-31: it saves time for the participant and the interviewer; it is transcribed afterwards).

"I am a Software Engineering student at FMAT working on a course project to improve the boutique's sales and inventory system. I would like to ask you some questions about your work; it takes 30–45 minutes. Your answers are used only for this course project. If you agree, I would like to record **only the audio** of the interview, so I do not have to write everything down while we talk; if you prefer that I do not record, I will only take notes. I will not record your name: in the notes and the transcript you appear only by a code. The recording is kept in my private files and deleted as soon as I have transcribed it; the notes are deleted when the course ends. Only anonymized summaries go into the project repository. You can stop, or ask me to stop the recording, at any time without giving a reason. If you have questions later, you can contact me or the course professor. Is that OK?"

## A. Current inventory process — CDU (H-04, H-07, H-17)
1. How are new products registered today? Who does it and with what tool?
2. How are products sent to the Sociales and Matemáticas points? Who records it? *(Q-07)*
3. How do you know how much stock each point has? How often do counts not match?
4. How are codes assigned to products? *(Q-08)*
5. *(CDU)* Which tools do you use for this work (paper, spreadsheets, a system)? Could you show me how you would add a new product to that tool? *(observe without helping; H-07)*
6. *(CDU)* Who attends customers at the CDU point? Is it the same person who registers products? *(H-17, Q-11, Q-17)*

## B. Payments and accounts receivable — Central Administration and client (H-08, H-10, H-11, H-13, H-16)
7. What does Central Administration mainly do with the boutique: query, sell, or both? From which stock? *(Q-02)*
8. How are card payments charged: separate terminal or integrated? *(Q-06)*
9. Who follows up on Interuady notes and how are they marked as paid? *(Q-09)*
10. What happens when an Interuady note has missing data or is paid late?
11. *(Central Administration)* Where do you usually work: at a desk with a computer, from a phone, or both? Which programs do you use every day? *(H-16)*
12. *(Central Administration)* Could you show me how you would look up a figure in a spreadsheet you use? *(observe without helping; H-16)*
