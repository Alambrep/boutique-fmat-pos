# Instrument — Staff and client interview guide (V-03, V-04, V-06)

> Semi-structured interview, 30–45 min, with CDU staff, Central Administration and the client. Administered in Spanish; this is the reference version.

## Consent script
Same as the [observation guide](observation-guide.md). Participant code: `CDU-__` / `ADM-__` / `CLI-01` · Date: ______

## A. Current inventory process — CDU (H-04, H-07)
1. How are new products registered today? Who does it and with what tool?
2. How are products sent to the Sociales and Matemáticas points? Who records it? *(Q-07)*
3. How do you know how much stock each point has? How often do counts not match?
4. How are codes assigned to products? *(Q-08)*

## B. Payments and accounts receivable — Central Administration and client (H-08, H-10, H-11, H-13)
5. What does Central Administration mainly do with the boutique: query, sell, or both? From which stock? *(Q-02)*
6. How are card payments charged: separate terminal or integrated? *(Q-06)*
7. Who follows up on Interuady notes and how are they marked as paid? *(Q-09)*
8. What problems have you had with Interuady notes (missing data, late payment)?
