**Why the requirements changed in delivery 2.** No field research could be done (see the [validation plan](../02-research/validation-plan.md#7-delivery-2-status-of-the-activities)), so **no change comes from user findings** and no hypothesis changed status. The changes come from two sources:

1. **Writing rules** (§2): statements that could not be verified or that used ambiguous or technical words.
2. **Designing the prototype** (v1): building the screens made several behaviors explicit that the delivery 1 text left open. Each of these cites the screen (`SCR`), design implication (`DI`) or guideline (`G`) it comes from.

Targets of the NFRs stay **tentative**; they are calibrated when field data and test results exist.

## 2. Writing rules applied

Based on NASA's guidance on writing good requirements `[R41]`:

| Rule | Example of what it removed |
|---|---|
| One subject and one predicate: *the system shall* + one observable behavior | FR-16: how quantities change and what happens below 1 were implicit |
| Verifiable: it can be inspected, demonstrated or tested | NFR-14 *"opens quickly"*; NFR-03 *"a small number of taps"*; FR-37 *"usable"* |
| No unverifiable terms (*quickly*, *usable*, *easy*, *adequate*…) or ambiguous ones (*etc.*, *and/or*) `[R41]` | NFR-08 *"rate the app as easy to use"* |
| The same words as the interface (UI labels in Spanish, in italics) and no technical terms in what the user sees (G-04) | FR-11 *"synchronization time"*; FR-31 availability labels |

## 5. Usability attributes (rubric 1c)

- **System level:** a new [usability specification](non-functional-requirements.md#0-usability-specification) states, for each of Nielsen's five attributes `[R1]`, what it means for this system, who it matters most for, at which level it applies and which NFRs operationalize it, and relates them to the ISO 9241-11 measures `[R2]`. It also sets the priority (learnability and errors first) used to choose the NFRs of the usability test.
- **Requirement level:** every NFR keeps its attribute and gains the column **Applies to**: *System* (e.g. NFR-09 touch targets, NFR-10 contrast, NFR-12 Spanish) or the FRs it constrains (e.g. NFR-06 → FR-20, FR-21; NFR-27 → FR-25).

## 7. What did not change, and why

- **Hypothesis-based requirements** (marked **(H)**) keep that mark: no hypothesis was validated or refuted.
- **Tentative targets** keep their values: there is no field baseline (V-01, V-05 blocked) and no test with users yet.
- **Deferred requirements** FR-D1…FR-D6 stay deferred: Q-02, Q-05, Q-06, Q-07, Q-09 and Q-10 are unanswered.
